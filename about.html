The studio behind Efie by Bisa

Wallin Labs is a two-person studio — Julia Wallin and Roland Danso — currently fully focused on building Efie by Bisa, a trust-first rental marketplace for Ghana. We're building the platform people in Ghana deserve.

We started out building AI document-intelligence tools — FundMemo, Surfaced, and Ryder — for real clients. That work is done; we're now all-in on Efie by Bisa.

What we believe

The product should fit how people actually transact

Not the other way around. We design around how landlords and renters in Ghana actually operate, not around what's easiest to build.

The best tool is one people actually need

Trust beats features every time. We design for the person taking the risk.

People should stay in control

Landlords and renters own their listings, their data, and their decisions. The platform earns trust — it doesn't lock people in.

Complexity should be invisible

Renting a home shouldn't feel complicated. The verification and trust work happens behind the scenes.
